Requirement (Cody, 2026-11-27): a full ptychography target needs JWST, Euclid, polarization and **weak lensing** (the ptychography maps the dark matter, so wide-field shear data around the cross are required, not only the strong-lens images). Scripts: `survey_crosses.py` (full), `survey_crosses_fast.py`, `survey_crosses_six.py`, `survey_crosses_nrao.py`; outputs `crosses_fast.log`, `crosses_six.log`, `crosses_nrao.log`.
