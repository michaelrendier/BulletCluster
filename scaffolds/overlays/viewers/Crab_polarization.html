Crab Nebula: HST polarization field (ACS F606W)

HST ACS/WFC F606W through POL0V/60V/120V (2003): ideal-polarizer Stokes, p debiased, 1.6-arcsec cells. Position-angle zero point ASSUMED (V3 offset -38.15 deg); instrumental polarization (~2%) not corrected; NICMOS (1997) agrees with ACS to 9.8 deg rms after one fitted offset. Background: JWST NIRCam composite (only the HST field has polarization data).
Harpoon = orientation, drawn pointing toward the top of the image (the data are axial: the head side is a drawing convention). Length ~ amplitude. Faint lines = field lines of the smoothed orientation field. Fainter vectors = filled from neighbouring cells where too few objects fall in a cell. Wheel = zoom, drag = pan, double-click = fit data, F = full image.
