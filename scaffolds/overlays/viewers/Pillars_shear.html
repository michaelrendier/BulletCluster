Pillars of Creation: JWST orientation (shear-measurement) field

JWST NIRCam F200W object orientations (41,270 objects), uncalibrated. NOT a lensing detection: the strongest signal is one global orientation lying along the mosaic's pixel x-axis (111 sigma; 54% of objects within 20 deg), an instrument/processing signature shared with the Crab and HE 0435 fields. See BulletCluster/TODO.md.
Harpoon = orientation, drawn pointing toward the top of the image (the data are axial: the head side is a drawing convention). Length ~ amplitude. Faint lines = field lines of the smoothed orientation field. Fainter vectors = filled from neighbouring cells where too few objects fall in a cell. Wheel = zoom, drag = pan, double-click = fit data, F = full image.
